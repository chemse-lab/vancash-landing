~215 s · 16x9 only · music: synthesized lo-fi bed (bpm chosen after the VO is cut, so sections land on downbeats) · VO: docs/script.md (12 takes, one per section)

Timing is driven by the voice-over: each VO section starts on a bar line, and every on-screen
event is keyed to the word that names it (Scribe word timestamps), then snapped to the nearest
beat or half-beat. Times below are estimates until the takes exist.

Look: brand tokens from the landing page. Paper #f6f9ff, ink navy #0c234a, one accent blue #2457f5,
plus the brand's own lime highlighter (#c5f85c) under the key word of each headline, as on the
site's h1. Display face Cairo (the app's own face), UI face IBM Plex Sans Arabic. All Arabic is set
right-to-left as whole words/lines (never per letter, so letters stay joined).
Screens: only the 10 real screenshots from the landing page (assets/shots/). Diagrams are pure
shape + type (device outlines, cloud, check marks); no invented app screens.
